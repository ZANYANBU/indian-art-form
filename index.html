      About this map

      Tracing the geography of Indian art

      Art in India grew out of specific places: a basalt cliff above a river, a delta rich in rice and clay, a desert court with money to spend on painters. This map plots eight of those places and explains what was made there, by whom, and why it mattered.

      Objective (CO1): to explore the geographic spread and influence of different art styles across India.

      How to use

      • Click a marker or a card in the list to fly to that place and open its story.
      • Use the filters to show only cave art, temples, folk painting or court arts and crafts.
      • "Take the tour" visits every location in sequence.
      • Keyboard: ← → to move between places, Esc to close.

      Built with

      Leaflet 1.9 and OpenStreetMap tiles. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no backend.
