About this project

Why a timeline?

Indian art is not one story but many overlapping ones. Placing artifacts on a single axis makes it easy to see continuities, such as the lost-wax casting that links the Dancing Girl of Mohenjo-daro with a Chola Nataraja three and a half millennia later, and ruptures, such as the arrival of Persian miniature techniques at the Mughal court.

Each artifact card opens a panel with its date, place, material, historical context and significance, plus a short "did you know" note for presentations.

How to use

  1. Use the era chips at the top to jump between periods.
  2. Click an artifact to open its detail panel.
  3. Use ← → keys (or the arrows in the panel) to move between artifacts.
  4. Press Esc to close.
